Study mode recommended first

Check each answer as you go and read the explanation immediately, while the question is still in your head. No time pressure. This is the mode that teaches.

Exam mode simulation

120-minute countdown, no feedback until you submit — the same conditions as the real thing. Use it once you have worked through study mode, to find out what survives time pressure.

Before you start

  • Select-2 items. 8 questions ask for exactly two answers. They are marked. Both must be right to score — and the two are usually complementary layers, not two phrasings of the same idea. If your picks are near duplicates, one is wrong.
  • Scenarios repeat. Several questions share a business context. Read the scenario carefully the first time; after that you can skim it.
  • Scoring. Scaled 100–1000 like the real exam. 720 passes, which is about 42 of 60. Aim for 850+ here before you book.
  • Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab mid-attempt and come back. In exam mode the clock keeps running.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 to select, ←→ to move, F to flag, Enter to check in study mode.
